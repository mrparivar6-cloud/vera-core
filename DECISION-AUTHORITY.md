# Decision & Authority Boundary

This document formally defines the decision and authority boundaries
of the VERA system.

## No Decision Authority

VERA does not make decisions.
VERA does not recommend actions.
VERA does not evaluate outcomes.
VERA does not initiate execution.

VERA only records, timestamps, and references informational inputs.

## No Operational Control

VERA does not control agents, bots, traders, or automated systems.

Any system that consumes VERA references operates independently and
assumes full responsibility for interpretation and execution.

## Separation of Responsibility

Decision-making, judgment, execution, and risk are explicitly external
to VERA.

VERA functions solely as a reference and attribution layer.

## Legal Clarification

VERA is not an advisor, controller, or operator.
VERA provides no guarantees, predictions, or assurances.

All authority remains with the human or system utilizing the reference.
